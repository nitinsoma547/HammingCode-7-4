## Campaign theme

Three hooks driving the month, anchored on one festival:

1. **Akshaya Tritiya** (Fri May 19) — the hero hook. Kerala-leaning
   sadya at $24/plate, two seatings of 30 plates each. The email,
   the flyer, the WhatsApp broadcast, one Reel and 3 social posts
   all echo this.
2. **Mother's Day** (Sun May 10) — secondary US-calendar hook.
   Single social post + a GBP event post. No upcharge, no pre-fixe —
   intentionally not a brunch gimmick because this audience eats
   regular South Indian Sunday lunch, not pre-fixe brunch.
3. **Indian wedding season** (May–June peak) — small-tray catering
   inquiry post. Aditi's isn't a full caterer in v1; this signals
   availability for 30–60 person tray pickups.

The email anchors on **Akshaya Tritiya** (Tue May 12, 10am — 7 days
lead). The Reel lands on May 22 (dosa on the tava — most viewable
food-process moment for this kitchen). The flyer is the
Akshaya-Tritiya-sadya banana-leaf hero.

## What's different from ChefScape (for diff readers)

ChefScape was the proof that the team flexes outside our default
desi market. Aditi's IS our default market — the diaspora-specific
moves are visible in:

- **whatsapp.txt** — ChefScape has no WhatsApp; Aditi's does, because
  the audience IS WhatsApp-native (`channels_in_scope.whatsapp_broadcast = true`).
- **Hero hook** — ChefScape used Memorial Day weekend (broad US
  calendar). Aditi's uses Akshaya Tritiya (Kerala-leaning festival).
  Memorial Day is a one-line GBP "closed Mondays as usual" note.
- **Flyer cultural accent** — ChefScape used no cultural accent.
  Aditi's uses the `banana-leaf` SVG motif from
  `design/cultural-accents.ts` at 18% opacity.
- **Malayalam parallel-label** — present on the flyer
  (`അക്ഷയ തൃതീയ സദ്യ`) and on two social posts (May 6 ghee roast,
  May 12 Akshaya Tritiya). ChefScape is English-only.
- **Closed Mondays** — kitchen is closed every Monday; the calendar
  respects that and the Memorial-Day GBP post calls it out so
  customers don't drive in.

## Why this is in the repo (not just in Supabase)

Two reasons:
1. **Demonstrates the team end-to-end on the default target audience**
   — Saturday meeting + onboarding demos for future Loudoun desi SMBs.
2. **Versioning** — campaigns evolve month over month and git history
   shows who tuned which hook.

Once `api/` is fully wired and Supabase is live, monthly campaigns
become DB rows in `campaigns` + `campaign_assets`; these JSON files
are the shape those rows match.
