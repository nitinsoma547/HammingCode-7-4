# 90-Day Marketing Plan — Aditi Spice Depot / Aditi Kitchen

*CMO-produced. Reads `.agents/product-marketing.md` as the source of truth on positioning.*
*Drafted: 2026-05-16*

## The decision tree at the top

**One sentence:** The kitchen is the growth lever; the next 90 days is about making Aditi Kitchen findable and irresistible at near-zero cost, then earning a real marketing budget by showing it works.

**Three things I'm explicitly NOT doing yet (and why):**
1. **No paid ads in weeks 1–4.** Driving traffic to a broken-listing / dormant-IG / photo-less brand wastes money. Fix the assets first.
2. **No menu / pricing / operational recommendations to the owner yet.** Out of marketer scope and untrusted source. Earn credibility on marketing first.
3. **No "rebrand" or website overhaul.** The brand equity (500+ reviews, two-brand structure, delivery presence) is fine. The problem is *activation*, not *identity*.

## Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–2) — $0 budget

**Owner of phase: `growth-marketer`**

Goal: get the basics good so anything we do later actually compounds. Almost all of this is **before** publishing anything new.

### 1.1 Access audit (Day 1)
**Owner: you (the marketer)** — get from the business owner:
- Aditi Spice Depot Google Business Profile (manager access)
- Aditi Kitchen Google Business Profile (if it exists separately — find out)
- Instagram for both depot and kitchen
- Facebook for both
- DoorDash merchant portal access (read-only is fine)
- The website CMS for aditikitchen.com and aditispicedepotherndon.com (or the developer's contact)
- Confirm: does the kitchen do catering? Hours? Owner story?

**Deliverable:** a one-page "access status" doc — what you have, what you're blocked on. Hand to the owner so they unblock.

### 1.2 Google Business Profile fix — Aditi Kitchen (Owner: `seo-lead`)
This is the highest-ROI cheap move in the whole plan.

- **Verify Aditi Kitchen has its own GBP** listed as a *Restaurant* (NOT as part of the grocery listing). If not, claim/create one.
- Primary category: **South Indian restaurant**. Secondary: Vegetarian restaurant, Indian restaurant.
- Add: hours, phone (703-464-1775), menu link, all 5 delivery platform links, photos (see 1.3).
- Description: "Aditi Kitchen serves authentic South Indian vegetarian food — dosa, idli, uttapam, biryani, thali — inside Aditi Spice Depot in Herndon. Affordable, family-run, vegetarian-only."
- Add "Aditi Kitchen" as an alternate name on the Spice Depot GBP if a separate one cannot be created.
- Turn on GBP messaging if it's off.

### 1.3 Photo refresh (Owner: `content-marketer` + you)
- 20–30 high-quality photos of: the masala dosa, the Aditi Feast thali, chole bhature, idli, uttapam, the storefront, the kitchen counter, fresh produce, friendly staff (with permission), the dining area.
- Phone camera in good light is fine for V1. No professional shoot needed.
- Upload to **both** GBPs, both Facebook pages, both Instagram bios, the website if old photos exist.
- This single action will likely lift GBP profile views by 30–50% on its own (Google rewards photo-rich listings).

### 1.4 In-store cross-promotion (Owner: `growth-marketer`)
The 500+ grocery customers don't reliably know the kitchen is a real restaurant. Fix that at the register.

- Tabletop tent card on the counter: "Tried our kitchen? Dosa, thali, chole bhature — made fresh in back. [QR to menu]" — QR points to aditikitchen.com/menu.
- Window/door cling visible from the parking lot: "Aditi Kitchen — South Indian Vegetarian — Lunch served daily."
- A second QR sign at the register: "Order delivery — DoorDash · UberEats" — code points to the DoorDash listing for first-order discount.
- Receipt insert / register pitch: "Have you tried our kitchen?" — train staff to mention it.

Cost: <$50 for printing. Owner approval needed for signage but it's a yes-ask, not a no-ask.

### 1.5 Baseline measurement (Owner: `revops-analyst`)
Capture week-1 numbers so we know if anything we do matters.

- GBP weekly: profile views, search vs. map views, direction requests, calls.
- Both Instagram accounts: followers, weekly impressions, weekly profile visits.
- DoorDash: weekly order count and rating for Aditi Kitchen.
- Website: if Google Analytics is connected, weekly users; if not, ignore for now.
- **Owner-provided:** approximate weekly kitchen ticket count and weekly grocery transactions. This is the real number; everything else is a proxy.

**Phase 1 success criterion:** All assets accurate and photo-rich. Baseline numbers captured. Access secured. Nothing is "broken" anymore.

## Phase 2 — Activate organic (Weeks 3–6) — $0–$100 budget

**Owner of phase: `brand-social`**

Goal: get a steady drumbeat of organic visibility working on the channels we already have.

### 2.1 Instagram reactivation (Owner: `brand-social` + `content-marketer`)
- **Cadence:** 3 posts/week + 4 stories/week. Sustainable for a solo marketer.
- **Content mix (per week):**
  - 1× hero food shot (dosa, thali) with a short, warm caption — "Today's dosa." or "Aditi Feast on the counter." Less is more.
  - 1× behind-the-scenes (the kitchen at work, a hand pouring batter, fresh sambar) — Reels format, 7–15 seconds.
  - 1× "did you know?" — a small fact about the food, an ingredient from the spice store, a tradition. Educational.
- **Stories:** lunch menu of the day, customer-eating moments (with permission), a delivery countdown, repost any tagged customer post.
- **Bio:** "South Indian vegetarian kitchen inside Aditi Spice Depot · Herndon VA · DoorDash + pickup · 📍 600 Carlisle Dr"
- **One post a week is the Spice Depot's**: a new arrival, a featured spice, a seasonal vegetable. Lighter touch.

Content template library — `content-marketer` to produce 12 caption templates so you're not writing from scratch each time.

### 2.2 Google review momentum (Owner: `growth-marketer`)
Right now reviews accrue to the Spice Depot. The Kitchen needs its own review base.

- After verifying the kitchen has its own GBP: print a small "Liked your meal? Leave a review" card with a QR to the kitchen review link. Drop in to-go bags.
- Train staff: "If a customer says it was great, hand them a card."
- Target: 20 new kitchen-specific reviews in 4 weeks. That's enough to move the listing visibly.
- Do not offer incentives for reviews — against Google's rules and a fast track to deletion.

### 2.3 GBP weekly posts (Owner: `seo-lead`)
- Both listings: 1 post per week. ("Today's special: chole bhature." "Catering available — call us." "Visit Aditi Kitchen for lunch.")
- These also feed into Google search results — small but compounding.

### 2.4 On-site SEO pass for aditikitchen.com (Owner: `seo-lead`)
- Title tag should be: "Aditi Kitchen — South Indian Vegetarian Restaurant in Herndon, VA"
- Meta description should mention: South Indian, vegetarian, dosa, thali, Herndon, lunch, delivery.
- Add structured data (schema.org/Restaurant) — see the `schema` skill for template.
- Add a clear "Order Delivery" section linking to all 5 platforms.
- Add a "Catering" page if catering is offered (confirm with owner first).
- Internal link from aditispicedepotherndon.com to aditikitchen.com prominently.

**Phase 2 success criteria (end of week 6):**
- 12+ IG posts published, follower count up
- Kitchen GBP has 15+ new reviews
- aditikitchen.com is indexed and ranking for "south indian restaurant herndon" page 1 or 2
- DoorDash weekly orders up 10–25% vs week 1 baseline (proxy for awareness lift)

## Phase 3 — Pitch the owner + paid pilot (Weeks 7–12) — budget-dependent

**Owner of phase: `performance-marketer`**

Goal: use the visible Phase 1–2 wins to negotiate a real marketing budget, then run two tightly-scoped paid experiments to prove the channel.

### 3.1 The owner pitch (Week 7) (Owner: you, supported by all)
A one-page memo to the business owner. Three sections:
1. **What we did in 60 days, $0 spent.** Before/after numbers from `revops-analyst`.
2. **What we learned.** E.g., "Kitchen GBP views grew 80% — there's real local intent." Or "IG engagement is mostly other Indians — non-Indian reach needs paid."
3. **What we want to try next, three budget tiers:**
   - **Lean: $200/mo** → $5/day Google Ads on "south indian restaurant herndon" / "vegetarian indian lunch herndon" only.
   - **Standard: $600/mo** → Google + Meta lunch-targeting ads to Herndon office workers; small DoorDash sponsored placement.
   - **Push: $1,500/mo** → Above + a catering-focused campaign + neighborhood mailers.

This memo is what gets you paid as a marketer. It's also `sales-enablement` work — the `product-marketer` will help structure it.

### 3.2 Paid pilot — only if budget approved (Owner: `performance-marketer`)
- Start with **one channel, two ad variants.** Google Search ads first (highest intent — someone searching "south indian restaurant herndon" is ready to eat).
- Geo-fence 5-mile radius of 600 Carlisle Dr.
- Two campaigns: "Lunch dine-in / pickup" and "Delivery via DoorDash."
- Set kill criteria up front: if cost-per-click > $X or cost-per-order > $Y after 2 weeks, kill and reassess.
- Conversion tracking must be live before launch — `revops-analyst` owns this.

### 3.3 Aditi Kitchen page CRO (Owner: `cro-specialist`)
With paid traffic coming, the destination has to convert. Audit:
- Is the "order now" CTA above the fold?
- Is there visible social proof (review count, star rating)?
- Is the menu one click away or buried?
- Mobile experience first — most lunch decisions happen on phones.

### 3.4 Catering motion (Owner: `growth-marketer` + `product-marketer`) — only if confirmed available
If the kitchen does catering: this is the single highest-margin opportunity for the business.
- Build a simple catering inquiry landing page with form.
- Outreach to Herndon-area tech companies (HR / office manager / EA contacts).
- Cross-listing on Indian community Facebook groups for events / weddings / pujas.
- Offer a "first catering order discount" to seed reviews.

**Phase 3 success criteria (end of week 12):**
- Owner has approved an ongoing monthly marketing budget.
- One paid channel is running with positive ROAS or a documented decision to kill.
- Kitchen weekly orders up 30–50% vs week 1 baseline across channels.

## What I would cut if budget were halved

If you have to do less, do this:
1. Keep: GBP fix for Aditi Kitchen (1.2)
2. Keep: Photo refresh (1.3)
3. Keep: In-store cross-promotion (1.4)
4. Keep: One IG post per week minimum (2.1)
5. Cut: Aditi Kitchen catering motion (3.4) until owner asks for it
6. Cut: Meta ads in Phase 3, run Google Search only
7. Cut: GBP weekly posts to monthly

The first four are 80% of the lift at <$100 total cost.
